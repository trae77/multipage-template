AI lead responder for local trades (simulated)

This shows what the n8n workflow in lead-responder/ does: a new lead gets an instant reply, two or three qualifying questions, an open booking time, and the owner gets an alert. Pick a scenario and press Play.

Conversation

    Workflow steps

    1. Lead received
    2. Consent checked
    3. Instant reply
    4. Qualifying questions
    5. Booking time offered
    6. Owner alert

    Replies here are scripted. In the workflow they come from an LLM step when a key is set, or a fixed template when it is not. Texts only go out when the person ticked the SMS box on the form, which is unchecked by default. Booking times come from a stub list in this demo.
