Privacy Policy

Template. The business must review and edit this page before the site goes live.

This page explains what Sample Photography Co. collects through this website and how it is used.

What we collect

Only what you type into a form here: your name, phone, email, address and the details of your request or order. If you create an account we keep your email and a hashed password. We don't use analytics or advertising trackers.

Why we collect it

To reply to you, give an estimate, schedule or complete the work or order you asked for, and keep normal business records. Nothing else.

Who we share it with

Our website host and the email service that delivers form messages to us. Payment details, if any, go straight to the payment provider and never touch this site. We don't sell or rent your information or share it for marketing.

Text messages

We only text you if you ask us to or check a box agreeing to it. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. Message and data rates may apply.

How long we keep it

As long as needed for your request and our records, then we delete it.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete what you sent us, contact hello@example.com or (555) 010-0100.

Outside content

Some pages load fonts or a map from outside providers, who may see your IP address as with any website.
